physical renders each note from a model of the instrument instead of an oscillator and an envelope: plucked strings use the extended Karplus‑Strong algorithm (Jaffe & Smith 1983), the piano uses stiff‑string partials at fn = n·f₀·√(1+Bn²) struck by a finite‑width hammer, and bars, tines and drum shells use modal resonator banks. Rendered voices are cached, so a repeated note is free.

humanize is the other half of not sounding like MIDI: per‑part feel offsets (the bass sits behind the ride, the hats push), Gaussian timing jitter, metric accents, and chords strummed rather than stacked. At 0 everything lands exactly on the grid at exactly the written velocity.

calibration — the band is matched to a reference recording by octave-band measurement, then a fixed EQ applies the difference. tone at 0 means "matches the reference".

Switching to classic restores the v0.0.1 sound everywhere — keyboard, drum grid, samples and jam — for an A/B.

Click ● rec in transport [ 01 ] to start recording the master output. Click again to stop and download. The file uses the format selected above.

the jam column lives inside [ 01 ]. type a progression — 1-1-1-1-4-4-1-1-5-4-1-5 for 12-bar blues, or C-G-Am-Em-F-C-F-G for canon — hit apply, then ▶ play. toggle drums / bass / middle / treble to strip the band down and play over it on [ 02 ].

click ● rec to capture the master bus, ↓ export to save the take, clear to throw it away (or, with nothing recording, to disarm the jam).
